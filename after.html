About Honeybee

Honeybee Bakery started in a home kitchen and grew into the neighborhood bakery we are today. We believe a bakery should smell like warm butter, know its regulars by name, and always have something sweet ready for the ride home.

Everything is baked in small batches through the morning — never all at once, never held too long.

How we bake

  • Small batches, all morning
  • Local honey in our signature bakes
  • Custom cakes made to order
