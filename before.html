About Us

Honeybee Bakery started in a home kitchen and grew into the neighborhood bakery we are today. We believe a bakery should smell like warm butter, know its regulars by name, and always have something sweet ready for the ride home. Everything is baked in small batches through the morning, so what you pick up is as fresh as we can make it.
